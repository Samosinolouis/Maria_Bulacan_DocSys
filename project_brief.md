# PROJECT BRIEF: PROPOSED CLOUD-BASED DOCUMENT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (DMS)
**Municipal Administrator's Office - Municipality of Santa Maria, Bulacan**

---

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

The proposed Document Management System (DMS) aims to digitalize, streamline, and modernize the handling of documents within the Municipal Administrator's Office. It will establish a centralized and secure platform for managing official records, ensuring faster turnaround of requests, accurate tracking, and reliable archiving.

The system will also support compliance with the mandated three-day processing period for simple requests under **Republic Act No. 11032** (*Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act*) while enhancing accessibility, transparency, and accountability in municipal operations.

In addition, the system will integrate a centralized meeting and event scheduling function to ensure efficient use of municipal venues, prevent conflicts, and improve coordination of schedules involving the Mayor and the Administrator.

---

## 2. OBJECTIVES

- Digitalize and systematically organize documents for quick retrieval and reference.
- Establish a seamless process for receiving, logging, transmitting, and closing requests and communications.
- Enable real-time monitoring of the status of documents (approved, endorsed, denied, pending).
- Enforce the three (3)-day maximum processing time for simple requests.
- Generate monthly and annual summary reports of actions taken by the Municipal Administrator's Office.
- Strengthen accountability by recording who received, transmitted, or acted upon each document.
- Provide centralized scheduling of meetings and events to avoid conflicts in venue usage and participant availability.
- Allow the Administrator and authorized staff to check schedules remotely via phone, with automated reminders for meetings and events.
- Promote transparency and efficiency in municipal administration services.

---

## 3. SCOPE OF WORK

### Core Functionalities

- **Digital Archiving:** Categorized storage of all essential documents (Executive Orders, SB Endorsements, Contracts, Permits, Memorandum Orders, Legal Advice, etc.).
- **Incoming Communication & Request Management:** Covers document reception, control number assignment, initial screening, preparation, review, approval/endorsement/denial, and final archiving.
- **Tracking & Monitoring:** Real-time tracking of the status of requests and communications.
- **Control Number Assignment:** Automatic or manual stamping of control numbers for proper logging.
- **Dashboards & Reports:**
  - **Metrics:** Total documents, incoming requests, pending actions, closed transactions.
  - **Monthly and Annual Summary Reports Covering:**
    - Executive Orders
    - SB Endorsements
    - Contracts/Agreements
    - Certifications
    - Permits
    - Memorandum Orders
    - Legal Advice
    - Work Endorsements
    - Communication Letter for National Agencies
    - Other communications and requests
- **Search & Retrieval:** Keyword, title, category, or control number-based searches. Inline PDF previews and offline downloads enabled.
- **Centralized Scheduling of Meetings and Events:**
  - Unified calendar system for meetings and official functions to avoid conflicts in schedule and venue use.
  - **Covers the following municipal venues:**
    - Municipal Conference Room
    - Command Center Room
    - Social Hall
    - Gymnasium
    - Mayor's Conference Room (special meetings)
    - Administrator's Office (special meetings)
  - **Functions:**
    - Schedule creation, editing, and cancellation with role-based access.
    - Venue booking and conflict detection.
    - Tagging of required attendees (Mayor, Administrator, other personnel).
    - Mobile access for quick schedule checking.
    - Automated reminders/notifications to attendees via the system.

### Compatibility

- Desktop-based (Windows) as the primary environment.
- Supports MS Word, Excel, and Adobe PDF.
- Scanner- and printer-friendly for converting paper to digital files.
- Accessible via web and mobile browsers for authorized users.

### Security & Compliance

- Role-based access control and authentication.
- Comprehensive audit logs.
- Compliance with RA 11032 and other recordkeeping regulations.

## 4. DOCUMENT WORKFLOW PROCESS

### Step 1 – Document Reception
- Clerk/Encoder receives incoming request (letters, proposals, permits, travel orders, venue/vehicle requests, food/overtime requests, endorsements, legal opinions, etc.).
- Control number assigned (e.g., Incoming No., Travel Order No., Overtime No.).
- Clerk logs request in both the system and physical logbook.
- Paper-based documents are scanned to PDF and uploaded.

### Step 2 – Initial Screening
- Clerk/Encoder checks completeness of attachments.
  - **Incomplete** $\rightarrow$ returned to client for compliance.
  - **Complete** $\rightarrow$ forwarded to Officer for preparation.

### Step 3 – Document Preparation
- Officer/Clerk drafts necessary documents:
  - Executive Orders
  - SB Endorsements
  - Memorandum Orders (Notice of Meeting, Personnel Memo, Office Memo)
  - Certifications & Permits
  - Endorsement Letters & Recommendations
  - Legal Opinions (endorsed to Legal Consultant)
  - Response letter
  - Letter request for National Agencies
- Drafts are saved and categorized in the system.

### Step 4 – Review & Approval
- Municipal Administrator and/or Executive Assistant II reviews drafts.
- **Options:** Approve, Endorse, or Deny.
- Approved/endorsed documents may proceed to the Mayor for signature if required.
- Denied requests are logged with reasons, and status updated.

### Step 5 – Transmission & Notification
- Clerk/Encoder transmits the approved/signed document to the requesting party or endorses it to the concerned office.
- Transaction details (receiving office/personnel, date, updated status) are logged.

### Step 6 – Completion & Archiving
- Once fulfilled (approved/endorsed/denied), the request is marked as **Closed**.
- Final signed copy scanned/uploaded into its category.
- Clerk logs the release of the document, including the recipient.
- Document remains accessible for reporting and future reference.

## 5. DOCUMENT COVERAGE

The DMS will cover the following document types:
- **Incoming Requests:** client requests, proposals, official letters.
- **Travel Orders:** requests for official travel with supporting approvals.
- **Venue Requests:** use of municipal facilities/venues.
- **Vehicle Requests:** use of municipal service vehicles.
- **Food Requests:** catering/food support for official functions.
- **Overtime Requests:** work beyond regular office hours.
- **Executive Orders:** official orders issued by the Mayor.
- **Contracts and Agreements:** contracts, MOAs, and related agreements.
- **SB Endorsements:** endorsements prepared for Sanggunian Bayan.
- **Memorandum Orders:** internal directives from Administrator/Mayor.
- **Certifications and Permits:** certifications, clearances, permits.
- **Legal Advice:** opinions/reviews from the Legal Consultant.
- **Communication for National Agencies:** request for project fundings and other concerns.
- **Endorsement Letters & Recommendations:** for work, medical, hospital, or financial assistance.

---

## 6. ROLE-BASED ACCESS CONTROL (RBAC)

- **Municipal Administrator (Approver):** Full access; reviews/approves/denies requests; manages users, reports, and settings.
- **Executive Assistant II (Approver):** Reviews, endorses, approves/denies requests; accesses dashboards and reports.
- **Officer:** Prepares documents (Executive Orders, endorsements, certifications, permits); contributes to reports.
- **Clerk/Encoder:** Receives, logs, scans/uploads documents; assigns control numbers; handles tagging, transmission, archiving; prepares simple response letters, permits, certifications, and endorsements; handles venue scheduling.

- Greater efficiency in preparing executive orders, endorsements, and certifications.
- **Optimized scheduling of meetings and events**, avoiding conflicts in venue and participant availability.

TECH STACK CONTEXT
  - Keycloak ............ authentication only (OIDC); no passwords in app DB
  - Shadow users ........ business DB mirrors Keycloak identities for fast,
                          customized querying; authorization via roles

================================================================================
1. SCOPE OF THE PROJECT
================================================================================

Project: A cloud-based Document Management System for the Municipal
Administrator's Office digitizing the six-step document lifecycle
(reception -> screening -> preparation -> review/approval -> transmission ->
archiving) with RA 11032 SLA enforcement and a centralized meeting/event
scheduling module.

1.1 IN SCOPE

  1.  Authentication & user management - Keycloak (OIDC) login; shadow user
      records synced on login; role assignment; activation/deactivation.
  2.  Role-based access control - permission model (Service:Action, wildcards)
      enforced at the service layer.
  3.  Document reception & intake - encoding of incoming requests, automatic
      control-number generation, multi-file upload of scanned documents and
      annexes, RA 11032 SLA deadline computation.
  4.  Initial screening - pass/fail with grounds, return-for-compliance loop,
      resubmission.
  5.  Document preparation - output documents (EOs, endorsements,
      certifications, permits, memos, legal opinions, response letters),
      officer assignment, draft uploads, statutory template library,
      submission for review.
  6.  Review & approval - approve / endorse / deny with mandatory written
      grounds; Mayor signature recording.
  7.  Transmission - dispatch records (recipient, office, method, proof of
      transmission upload).
  8.  Completion & archiving - final signed copy upload, archive folder
      filing, closing, permanent read-only retrieval.
  9.  Search & retrieval - full-text (inverted index) and metadata search;
      filtering; inline PDF preview; download.
  10. Dashboards & reports - workload metrics, SLA/overdue monitoring,
      monthly/annual summaries per document category, export.
  11. Audit trails - immutable per-module history (document_logs,
      activity_logs) with actor snapshots.
  12. Scheduling module - booking across the six municipal venues, conflict
      detection, attendee tagging (Mayor/Administrator), mobile-browser
      schedule viewing, reminders as in-app notifications.
  13. In-app notifications - notification center with unread tracking and
      deep links; NO email/SMS delivery in this release.
  14. Hosting - single VM (application server + database); MinIO object
      storage for files; daily off-site backups.

1.2 OUT OF SCOPE

  - Email/SMS notification delivery (in-app only; see Future Enhancements).
  - Replacement of the physical logbook (system runs parallel to it).
  - Cryptographic/PKI digital signatures - signature events are recorded;
    documents are not e-signed.
  - Scanner device integration - scanners produce files externally; the
    system receives uploads only.
  - Integration with external LGU systems (Sangguniang Bayan, Mayor's office
    systems, national agency portals).
  - Backlog digitization of historical records predating the system.
  - Citizen-facing portal / online submission - internal system; requesting
    parties are recorded as data.
  - Native mobile applications - mobile support is browser-based and limited
    to schedule checking.
  - High-availability / multi-node deployment - single VM by design.
  - Recurring event rules, RSVP tracking, external (non-user) attendees, and
    venue capacity management in the scheduling module.

1.3 FUTURE ENHANCEMENTS

  FE-01  Email/SMS notification gateway via a notification_deliveries table
         (notification_id, channel, status, sent_at, ...) fanning out from
         notifications; the existing table stays the source of truth.
  FE-02  Citizen-facing portal for online submission and status inquiry.
  FE-03  Cryptographic (PKI-based) digital signatures in place of recorded
         signature events.
  FE-04  Bulk digitization tooling for historical archive backlogs.
  FE-05  Automatic event creation from approved venue requests (soft
         event.source_request_id linkage between modules).

2. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

A. AUTHENTICATION & USER MANAGEMENT
  FR-01  Users shall log in via Keycloak (OIDC); the system shall store no
         local credentials.
  FR-02  Shadow user records shall be keyed by the Keycloak OIDC "sub" claim
         (users.id); created on first login and updated on subsequent logins
         when the Keycloak profile changes.
  FR-03  User deactivation shall be soft (is_active = false); user records
         shall never be deleted once referenced by any record or log.
  FR-04  Authorized administrators shall assign/deassign roles; each
         assignment shall record who assigned it and when.
  FR-05  Every service-layer action shall enforce its Service:Action
         permission, computed as the union of the actor's roles'
         permission_payload, supporting "Service:*" and "*:*" wildcards.
  FR-06  Administrators shall manage users and roles and view any user's
         effective permissions.

B. DOCUMENT RECEPTION (STEP 1)
  FR-07  Clerks shall encode incoming requests with type, subject, requesting
         party, origin office, channel, and priority.
  FR-08  The system shall issue unique sequential control numbers per type
         prefix and year (e.g., TO-2026-0045) atomically - concurrent clerks
         shall never collide or skip numbers.
  FR-09  The system shall compute sla_deadline = received_at + 3 business
         days, excluding weekends and holidays read from the holidays table
         (RA 11032).
  FR-10  Clerks shall upload scanned letters and annexes; accepted formats
         whitelisted (PDF, DOCX, XLSX) up to a configured size limit; a
         server-computed SHA-256 checksum and uploader identity shall be
         recorded. The system shall NEVER generate attachment content.
  FR-11  Reception shall automatically write a RECEIVED entry to
         document_logs with the acting user and control number.

C. INITIAL SCREENING (STEP 2)
  FR-12  Clerks shall record a screening decision (pass/fail) against a
         completeness checklist (attachments, addressing, signatures).
  FR-13  On failure the request shall enter RETURNED_FOR_COMPLIANCE with the
         specific deficiencies recorded.
  FR-14  Clerks shall attach resubmitted files to a returned request,
         returning it to screening.
  FR-15  On pass the request shall advance to PREPARATION with an audit
         entry.

D. DOCUMENT PREPARATION (STEP 3)
  FR-16  Officers (and clerks, for simple letters/permits/certifications)
         shall create one or more output documents linked to a request;
         standalone output documents (no request) shall be supported for
         initiative issuances (EOs, memorandum orders).
  FR-17  Each output document shall carry a category from document_types and
         its own sequential control number.
  FR-18  The system shall record the assigned drafting officer.
  FR-19  Drafters shall upload draft files, versioned as DRAFT attachments;
         drafts are authored externally (e.g., MS Word) and uploaded.
  FR-20  The system shall provide a statutory template library, browsable by
         category.
  FR-21  Submitting a draft for review shall set the document to UNDER_REVIEW
         and the request to REVIEW, with an audit entry.

E. REVIEW & APPROVAL (STEP 4)
  FR-22  Only roles holding review permissions (Municipal Administrator,
         Executive Assistant II) shall see and act on the review queue.
  FR-23  Approvers shall approve, endorse, or deny; denial shall REQUIRE
         written grounds (mandatory field).
  FR-24  Every decision shall record the decision-maker and timestamp;
         denial grounds shall be displayed on the document dossier.
  FR-25  The system shall record whether Mayor signature is required and
         capture the signature event (signatory, date).

F. TRANSMISSION (STEP 5)
  FR-26  Clerks shall record transmission details - recipient name, receiving
         office, received-by, method (pickup/courier/email), date-time - with
         multiple transmissions allowed per document.
  FR-27  Clerks shall upload proof of transmission (e.g., scanned signed
         receiving copy) linked to the transmission record.
  FR-28  Transmission shall set the request to TRANSMITTED with an audit
         entry.

G. COMPLETION & ARCHIVING (STEP 6)
  FR-29  Clerks shall upload the final signed copy (SIGNED_FINAL attachment)
         prior to closing; closing shall support filing the request's
         documents into an archive folder.
  FR-30  A request shall be closable from APPROVED, ENDORSED, DENIED, or
         TRANSMITTED; denied requests may close directly without transmission.
  FR-31  Closed records shall become read-only and permanently retrievable.

H. SEARCH & RETRIEVAL
  FR-32  The system shall provide full-text search over request/document
         metadata (title, control number, requesting party, office) and
         indexed document content via the inverted index.
  FR-33  Results shall be filterable by type, category, status, date range,
         and origin office.
  FR-34  Users shall preview PDFs inline and download permitted files; file
         access shall respect the same permission model.

I. DASHBOARDS & REPORTS
  FR-35  The dashboard shall display total documents, incoming requests,
         pending actions, closed transactions, and SLA-at-risk/overdue counts.
  FR-36  The system shall generate monthly and annual summary reports per
         output category (Executive Orders, SB Endorsements, Contracts/
         Agreements, Certifications, Permits, Memorandum Orders, Legal Advice,
         Work Endorsements, Communication Letters).
  FR-37  Reports shall be exportable to PDF and Excel.
  FR-38  The system shall flag requests whose SLA deadline has passed or is
         within a configurable warning window.

J. AUDIT TRAILS
  FR-39  Every state-changing operation shall write exactly one immutable
         audit entry (actor snapshot, action type, timestamp, payload) in the
         same database transaction as the change.
  FR-40  document_logs and activity_logs shall be append-only, viewable per
         request/document/event, and not editable or deletable through any
         interface.

K. SCHEDULING
  FR-41  Authorized users shall create, edit, and cancel meetings/events
         across the six municipal venues with role-based access; special-use
         venues (Mayor's Conference Room, Administrator's Office) shall
         require an elevated booking permission.
  FR-42  The system shall detect and prevent booking conflicts: venue-time
         overlap, required-attendee double-booking, and Mayor/Administrator
         double-booking; cancelled events shall not block slots.
  FR-43  Events shall support attendee tagging (system users) and
         Mayor/Administrator involvement flags.
  FR-44  Schedules shall be viewable via mobile browsers for authorized
         users.
  FR-45  Reminders shall be materialized as in-app notifications
         (EVENT_REMINDER), generated idempotently per user and event for
         events occurring within the next 24-48 hours.
  FR-46  Event creation, modification, and cancellation shall be recorded in
         activity_logs.

L. NOTIFICATIONS
  FR-47  The system shall generate in-app notifications for: drafts submitted
         for review (to approvers), decisions recorded (to requesting-side
         staff), event reminder/updated/cancelled (to attendees), and SLA
         at-risk/overdue (to responsible staff).
  FR-48  Users shall have a notification inbox showing unread count and
         items newest-first.
  FR-49  Users shall mark notifications as read individually or all at once.
  FR-50  Notifications shall deep-link to their source entity (request,
         document, or event).
  FR-51  Notification delivery channels shall be in-app only in this release
         (no email/SMS).

3. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

3.1 PERFORMANCE
  NFR-01  Dashboard and queue pages shall load in <= 3 s (p95) under normal
          office load.
  NFR-02  Keyword search shall return in <= 2 s over a corpus of 100,000
          documents via the inverted index.
  NFR-03  The system shall support at least 50 concurrent authenticated users.
  NFR-04  Foreign keys, status, control numbers, dates, and hot-path composites
          (venue_id + event_date; user_id + created_at) shall be indexed;
          document text shall carry the full-text/inverted index.

3.2 SECURITY
  NFR-05  All authentication shall be delegated to Keycloak (OIDC); no
          passwords shall be stored in the application database.
  NFR-06  All traffic shall be TLS-encrypted; MinIO access shall use
          application-scoped credentials server-side only, with downloads
          delivered via short-lived presigned URLs.
  NFR-07  Authorization shall be enforced server-side at the service layer;
          client-side hiding of controls shall not be considered a security
          control.
  NFR-08  Every uploaded file shall have a server-computed SHA-256 checksum
          stored for integrity verification.
  NFR-09  document_logs and activity_logs shall be append-only at the database
          grant level (no UPDATE/DELETE).
  NFR-10  The system shall comply with RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act): role-scoped
          visibility of personal data and a defined retention policy.
  NFR-11  Sessions shall be governed by Keycloak policy (idle/absolute
          timeouts, logout propagation).
  NFR-12  User identity lifecycle: shadow users are keyed by the Keycloak
          "sub" and deactivated, never deleted, preserving referential
          history.

3.3 RELIABILITY & BACKUP
  NFR-13  Target availability of 99% during office hours (single-VM
          deployment by design).
  NFR-14  Database backups shall run at least daily with off-site copies in
          MinIO; RPO <= 24 h, RTO <= 4 h.
  NFR-15  File durability shall rely on MinIO retention/versioning; checksum
          reconciliation shall detect orphaned or corrupted objects.

3.4 USABILITY & COMPLIANCE
  NFR-16  The primary interface shall target desktop Windows workstations
          (>= 1280 px); mobile browsers shall support schedule viewing only.
  NFR-17  The interface shall follow DICT government web template compliance,
          including proper RA 8491 heraldic usage.
  NFR-18  All timestamps shall use Philippine Standard Time (RA 10535) and
          display consistently.
  NFR-19  The system shall support MS Word, Excel, and PDF formats and
          printable official forms (routing slips, transmittals).
  NFR-20  The system shall support modern evergreen browsers (current and
          prior major version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox).

3.5 MAINTAINABILITY
  NFR-21  Permission strings shall be defined once as code constants and
          validated on write (Service:Action, wildcards).
  NFR-22  Database schema changes shall ship via versioned migrations; no
          manual DDL in production.
  NFR-23  The SLA holiday calendar shall be maintained as data (holidays
          table), not hard-coded dates.
  NFR-24  Module boundaries shall be enforced: no foreign key shall cross the
          Document Module / Booking Module boundary; both shall share only the
          platform group (users, roles, notifications).
